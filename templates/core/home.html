Features, Healthcare Challenges & Our Solutions

Explore how the Healthcare Analytics & Prediction Platform (HAPP) solves critical healthcare delivery challenges through intelligent technology.

Predictive Health Risk Assessment

Evaluates vital health metrics (Blood Pressure, Heart Rate, SpO2, BMI, Blood Glucose) to classify patient health into Low, Medium, and High risk categories.

Digital e-Prescription (e-Rx) System

Allows doctors to create multi-medicine digital prescriptions with dosage, frequency, and duration presets, printable as hospital-grade PDFs with verification QR codes.

Role-Based Password Authentication

Production-grade password authentication backed by 5-attempt brute-force lockout safeguards.

Conflict-Free Appointment Scheduling

Real-time doctor slot availability checker, automated rescheduling workflows, 3-hour email reminders, and interactive cancellation modals.

Fragmented Medical Records & Paper Loss

Patients often misplace paper prescriptions and lab reports, leading to repeated tests, inconsistent treatments, and missing medical histories.

Delayed Identification of High-Risk Conditions

Without automated risk calculation, subtle progression in blood pressure or blood glucose indicators goes unnoticed until complications arise.

Handwritten Prescription Errors & Forgery

Illegible handwriting causes pharmacy dispensing errors, while paper prescriptions lack digital verification against unauthorized alterations.

Data Breach & Unauthorized Access Vulnerabilities

Traditional single-password portals leave sensitive health records exposed to credential stuffing, brute-force attacks, and session hijacking.

Solution 1
Unified Digital Health Vault

Centralized database storing prescriptions, lab reports, vitals, and consultation history accessible anytime to authorized patients and attending doctors.

Solution 2
Automated Clinical Risk Scoring

Algorithmic risk evaluation alerts doctors to elevated vital signs instantly, prioritizing high-risk patients for urgent clinical intervention.

Solution 3
QR-Verified e-Prescriptions

Generates clean, legible, computer-authorized e-Prescriptions with embedded verification QR codes to eliminate dispensing errors and forgery.
